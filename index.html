Within Reach

A seated, hands-only game that learns your reach, your hands and your steadiness in 90 seconds, then builds every challenge to fit you.

Sit comfortably and put your controllers down: you play with your bare hands, any finger works. A session takes about 6 minutes. Everything runs on your headset; nothing is uploaded.

Desktop preview: click (or click and drag) to poke. Esc to leave.
